## English (en-US)

**App name (29):** Match Baller: Football Trivia

**Short description (79):**
Name a player who played for both clubs before your rival. Live football duels!

**Full description:**

Think you know football? Prove it.

Match Baller puts you head-to-head with real opponents. You each pick a club, two crests appear and the countdown starts: 3… 2… 1… The first to type a player who wore both shirts takes the round. First to 3 rounds wins the match.

⚽ TWO GAME MODES
• Club – Club: Real Madrid and Chelsea? Rüdiger! Barcelona and Liverpool? Your move.
• Country – Club: one picks a country, the other a club. England and Real Madrid? Bellingham!

🌍 HUGE DATABASE
• 5,600+ clubs, 200+ countries, 90,000+ players
• Club crests and player photos
• Career data updated automatically every day

🏆 LEADERBOARDS AND LEAGUES
• Every win earns trophies — climb from Amateur to Legend
• World and weekly leaderboards
• Win 3 in a row for streak bonuses

⚡ FAST AND SMOOTH
• Player suggestions with photos as you type
• Matched in seconds, any time
• Pass vote: if you both pass, a new round starts

🎉 SHARE WITH FRIENDS
Send your win to WhatsApp or Instagram in one tap and challenge them to a rematch.

English, Turkish, Spanish and German. No ads.

Football data from Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons.
